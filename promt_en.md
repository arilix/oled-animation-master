# 2D Lyric Video Prompt - 1.3 Inch LCD Version

## Main Prompt (Ready to Use)
Create a **2D flat-illustration lyric video** specifically for a **1.3 inch LCD** with a **romantic, calm, and hopeful** mood. Use a **static camera** for the full duration.

Use a **240x240 px canvas** (top priority for 1.3 inch ST7789 LCD). If the panel differs, scale proportionally without changing the core composition.

Split the screen into two sections:
- **left 45%** for rose growth animation,
- **right 55%** for lyrics.

On the left side, show a simple illustrated rose that grows **slowly and smoothly** throughout the video as a symbol of emotional healing and a new beginning, through these stages: **seed in soil -> small sprout -> small leafy plant -> taller plant -> rose bud -> fully bloomed rose**.

On the right side, display song lyrics **line by line** with small-screen readability rules. Each lyric line must:
- appear with a **soft fade in**,
- stay briefly for readability,
- then **fade out** smoothly as the next line appears.

Small LCD text rules:
- Maximum **1 active line** at a time.
- If a sentence is long, split it into short chunks (about 12-18 characters per visual line).
- Use a font size that remains readable at 240x240 (around 14-18 px, depending on the font).

Use elegant typography: a **soft serif** or **simple handwritten** style that remains readable on a small screen. Apply a **minimal monochrome 2D** visual style.

**Important directions:**
- 2D animation only.
- Flat illustration style.
- No 3D rendering.
- No realistic textures.
- No decorative background (no particles, no extra elements).
- Use a plain/clean background so only the rose and lyrics are visible.
- Motion must be soft, organic, unhurried, and consistently soothing.
- Minimize tiny details so visuals stay clear on a 1.3 inch display.
- **Use only 2 colors: black and white** (no grayscale and no other colors).

## Art Direction
- Mood: romantic healing, reflective, soft, hopeful.
- Color palette: **black (#000000)** and **white (#FFFFFF)** only.
- Text contrast: high enough for clear readability on a small screen.
- Animation rhythm: slow, synced with the emotional flow of the song.

## Production Notes (1.3 Inch LCD)
- Target resolution: **240x240 px**.
- Effective frame rate: **15-24 fps** for lighter performance.
- Lyric transition animation: soft ease-in-out, fade duration 0.25-0.5 seconds.
- Required palette: **2 colors only (black and white)**.
- Keep enough negative space so composition stays clean and minimal.
- Avoid extra decorative elements. Focus only on the rose and lyrics.
- Use clear, simple line shapes so visuals remain sharp on a small screen.

## Style Reference Summary
A minimalist 2D lyric video for a 1.3 inch LCD: soft motion, **black-and-white monochrome**, readable typography for small screens, no decorative background, and rose growth as the core emotional narrative.
